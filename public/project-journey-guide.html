Project Journey

Everything a new project needs, gathered once — guided step by step, with AI doing the heavy lifting whenever you have a document or a description to start from.

The short version

Creating a project sets up the foundation every assessment builds on — its classification, framework, privacy profile and technology stack. The wizard collects it in five focused steps instead of one long form, and lets you save a draft at any point and finish later.

You can start two ways: let AI pre-fill the form from a document or a plain-language description, or fill it in manually. Either way you land on the same Review screen before anything is created.

All fields are required to submit. A complete project profile is what makes the downstream control tailoring and evidence guidance accurate. If you don't have everything yet, Save as draft — the wizard keeps what you've entered and you can resume from the Projects list.
Step 0 — Choose your path

Two ways to start

AI-assisted · fastest

Start from a document or description

Upload an existing artefact, or describe the system in your own words. AI reads it and pre-populates every step for you to review.

  • Upload a document — PDF, Word, image or text. AI extracts the project details.
  • Describe it — a paragraph is enough; AI infers classification, PII, tech and more.
  • You review and correct — AI never submits for you.
Manual

Fill it in yourself

Prefer to type it out, or working from information only you hold? Go straight into the five steps.

  • Every field has inline guidance and sensible options.
  • Framework-specific fields adapt as you choose a framework.
  • Switch to AI assist at any time from Step 1.
Best of both: most people upload a document to get 80% pre-filled, then fine-tune the last details by hand before Review.

Never lose your place

Drafts: save now, finish later

Save at any step Choose Save as draft and the project is stored with everything entered so far. Nothing is validated as complete yet, so partial is fine.
Resume from Projects Drafts appear in your Projects list with a Draft badge. Open one to drop straight back into the wizard where you left off.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Does the AI submit the project for me?

No. AI only pre-fills the fields from your document or description. You always land on the Review screen and submit yourself — so nothing is created without your confirmation.

What file types can I upload for AI pre-fill?

PDF, Word, plain text and Markdown, and images (PNG/JPG) — for example an architecture doc, a prior assessment, or a screenshot of a system diagram. AI extracts what it can and leaves the rest for you.

The framework fields changed after I picked a framework — why?

That's by design. Baseline, category and applicability terms differ between frameworks (ITSG-33 vs. CIS Controls vs. ISO 27001, etc.), so the wizard shows the right terminology for the one you selected.

Do I have to fill in everything before I can save?

To submit, yes — all fields are required for an accurate profile. To save a draft, no — save whatever you have and complete it later from the Projects list.

Can I change these details after the project is created?

Yes. The project profile stays editable — but it's worth getting it right up front, since classification and framework choices shape the control baseline your assessments inherit.
