Workflow A — “Guided Rail”

One rail, always on screen, answers three questions at a glance: where am I, what is done, and what do I do next. Every page in a project keeps the same rail; the body changes. A single Next action card gives exactly one primary button, and a sticky commit bar at the bottom never lets a step end without telling you what it unlocked. Use the pills to walk the seven steps.

    What this design fixes

    • One journey, not two. The project’s 4 stages and the assessment’s 5 stages collapse into a single 7-step rail, so the two trackers can never disagree.
    • Exactly one primary button per screen. The “Next action” card is the only blue button; everything else is secondary.
    • “Waiting on” is explicit. The rail names the person who owes the next move, so the project stops feeling abandoned after it is sent out.
    • Steps complete when you complete them. Save tailoring finishes tailoring; scoring controls advances the audit — not a side effect of a different button.
    • Nothing ends silently. The sticky commit bar states what the action will do and what is still missing before you press it.
