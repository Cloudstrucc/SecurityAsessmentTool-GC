Decision

An interim authorization to operate (iATO) is granted for Member Services Portal, effective 2026-08-05 and expiring 2027-08-04, subject to the 6 conditions recorded in section 4.

Executive summary

The Member Services Portal has completed a full ITSG-33 PBMM assessment covering 230 applicable controls. 196 controls are satisfied, 24 are partially satisfied and 10 are not satisfied. The residual risk is assessed as MODERATE and is concentrated in identity lifecycle automation and container patch currency. No finding exposes personal information to unauthenticated access.

Residual risk statement

Residual risk is MODERATE. The two high-risk findings (AC-2(3), SI-2) are operational rather than architectural: both have compensating controls in place — privileged sessions are time-boxed and logged, and build-time dependency scanning blocks known-vulnerable code at merge. The realistic worst case is a delayed detection of an unused privileged account being reused, which the audit pipeline would surface within 24 hours.

Decision rationale

An interim authorization is granted because the system delivers a material service benefit, the control posture is strong in the areas that protect personal information at rest and in transit, and the outstanding findings are bounded, owned and scheduled. Full authorization is withheld until every condition below is accepted.

Conditions of authorization

Full ATO will be granted when all six POA&M items reach the ACCEPTED state, and in any case no later than the expiry date below. Any item that slips its deadline must be re-baselined with a written justification approved by the authorizing official.
