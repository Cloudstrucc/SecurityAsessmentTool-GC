Why am I being asked to approve this?

✕

Your organization asks for approval before every AI-generated report, so nobody spends the shared token allowance without seeing the cost first.

The alternative — a standing threshold

An administrator can switch this off in favour of a standing threshold: reports estimated under the threshold generate immediately with no dialog, and only the expensive ones stop to ask. You keep a running total either way, and you are always told what a report actually cost afterwards.

SettingWhat you would see

Standing threshold — e.g. 25,000 tokens Nothing under 25,000. This dialog only above it.
Your personal cap is separate and always applies. A standing threshold changes when you are asked; it never lets you exceed your own monthly token cap, and it never removes the organization's allowance limit.

Only the organization's primary administrator can change this (Settings → AI & automation → Cost controls). You are signed in as a project user.
